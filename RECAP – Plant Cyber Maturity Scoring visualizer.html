Scoring methodology

Final score (0–5) = Σ (weight × score) ÷ 100, where weights total 100 and each parameter is scored 0–5. Percentage = final ÷ 5.

Per-parameter score: 0 Not in place · 1 Planned / draft only · 2 Documented, not implemented · 3 Partially implemented · 4 Implemented, documents signed by CISO · 5 Verified at site, signed by CISO, reviewed periodically.

Status: ≥4 Complied · 2–3 Partially complied · ≤1 Not complied. Severity of a gap: Must Have scoring 0–1 = Critical, 2 = High, 3 = Medium; Should Have gaps = Low.

Tiers: L1 Initial (<1.5) · L2 Basic (<2.5) · L3 Intermediate (<3.5) · L4 Advanced (<4.5) · L5 Leading (≥4.5). Gate: a plant scoring 0 on any Must Have parameter is capped at L3 until that gap is closed.

Weights reflect the checklist priority (Must Have vs Should Have) and the impact of the control on OT safety: monitoring, firewall and segmentation, access control, SCADA and remote access carry the highest weights; advanced technologies (Should Have, within 1 year) carry the lowest.
